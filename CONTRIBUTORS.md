## 🤝 How to Get Listed

1. Pick an issue or submit a pull request for a bug fix, new feature, documentation improvement, or translation.
2. Once your PR is approved and merged, you can submit a quick PR adding yourself to the **Community Contributors** list above!
3. You will also be featured in our release notes and celebrated in our Discord community.
